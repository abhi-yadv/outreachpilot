# Draft review guide

Use this when testing OutreachPilot with the first 20–30 real leads you are allowed to contact, or with clearly labeled synthetic leads. Keep private names, email addresses, CVs and outreach history out of public GitHub issues and screenshots.

## Review each draft

Score each item from 0 (missing/incorrect) to 2 (clear and accurate):

| Criterion | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Company fact | The selected fact appears on the linked page and is still current |
| Opportunity | A listed role is current, or the open inquiry avoids claiming a vacancy |
| Relevance | The candidate's actual skill or project connects to that fact |
| Subject | Short, specific, accurate and readable without opening the email |
| Tone | Sounds like a person, avoids pressure and exaggerated promises |
| Clarity | Says who the candidate is, what internship they seek, and what reply they want |

Also record: total word count, number of edits before approval, whether the checker missed or incorrectly flagged wording, and whether the recipient/CV preview was correct. A draft is ready only after the human reviewer confirms every claim and recipient. Do not use an inbox-placement percentage as a quality score; the app cannot predict delivery.
